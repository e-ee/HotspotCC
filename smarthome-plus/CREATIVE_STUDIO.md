# Creative Studio 4.0

Open Light Studio and choose **Create · Direction · Sleep**.

- **Living Aurora:** layered jade curtains with indigo and violet, using the apartment map. The original Aurora remains available.
- **Paint & touch:** choose Paint, select a colour, and drag over the room. Brush reach controls the blend radius. Undo restores the previous painted state. Ripple sends a soft wave from a tap, with inward or outward travel.
- **Scene morph:** choose two compatible saved scenes, a duration, and optional spatial stagger. Missing scene members retain their starting look; off entries can fade to or from off. HSL scenes are supported; CT-only entries are omitted.
- **Journey composer:** add moods, saved variations or scenes; edit hold and dissolve times; reorder/delete chapters; name, save and loop the sequence.
- **Variations:** Shuffle retains the mode character while changing its colour arrangement. Save variation stores the settings in My moods.
- **Sleep descent:** 5, 15, 30, 60 or 120 active minutes. Movement slows, colour warms, brightness fades, and the sender finishes every selected bulb at zero before stopping. Pause also pauses the descent. Speed does not shorten the countdown.
- **Explore around this look:** keeps an original snapshot and offers warmer, cooler, deeper, softer and darker variations without accumulating repeated changes. Save a variation, or pause and save a real-readback scene.
- **Wave direction:** a 0–360 degree map angle, reverse travel, and inward/outward touch ripples. Saved moods remember direction and playback speed.

All effects share the existing round-robin vendor HSL sender, backpressure handling and 4–6 second native transitions. Ambient changes stay bounded. Hue drift follows the short path across the red seam. The visual map previews commanded colours; physical bulb output is not measured. Scene capture still saves only bulbs that actually reply.

Custom journeys and variations persist in Home preferences. Live timers and transient touch ripples are not restarted automatically after app termination. No microphone or fabricated audio reactivity is added.

Update over the installed Plus app. Keep the current app container and invitation identity. No bulb re-pairing is required.
